# Shepherd's Pie (Turkish-Style) with Lamb Meatballs

**Tags:** lamb, meatballs, mashed potato, leftovers, baked, Turkish-inspired  
**Serves:** 3–4  
**Time:** about 1 hour

A shepherd's pie built from raw lamb meatballs and leftover mashed potatoes. The meatballs are browned whole and baked in a thick sauce of pepper paste, cumin, Aleppo pepper and pomegranate molasses, under a yogurt-loosened mash finished with Aleppo butter and sumac.

---

## Ingredients

### Filling
- 450 g (1 lb) raw lamb meatballs, about 3 cm (halve or re-roll larger ones)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, finely diced
- 1 green pepper, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste + 1 tbsp pepper paste (biber salçası), or 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp Aleppo pepper (pul biber)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Pinch of cinnamon or allspice
- 1 cup (240 mL) chicken or lamb stock
- 1 tsp pomegranate molasses
- ½ cup peas (optional)
- Handful of parsley, chopped
- Salt and black pepper

### Topping
- 2–3 cups leftover mashed potatoes
- 2–3 tbsp yogurt or warm milk
- 1 egg yolk (optional, helps browning)
- ½ cup grated kaşar or mozzarella, or crumbled feta
- 1 tbsp butter, melted with ½ tsp Aleppo pepper
- Sumac, to finish

---

## Method

1. **Brown the meatballs.** Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Heat the oil in a wide skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the meatballs in a single layer, turning, for 5–6 minutes. They do not need to be cooked through. Lift them out and spoon off all but about 1 tbsp of fat.
2. **Cook the vegetables.** In the same pan, cook the onion, carrot and green pepper with a pinch of salt until soft, 6–8 minutes. Add the garlic, pastes, cumin, Aleppo pepper, oregano and cinnamon and cook for 2 minutes.
3. **Simmer the sauce.** Add the stock and pomegranate molasses, scraping up the browned bits. Simmer for 10–12 minutes, until properly thick. Stir in the peas and parsley and taste for salt.
4. **Coat the meatballs.** Return the meatballs to the pan and turn them in the sauce. Spread everything in a 2-litre (8-inch) baking dish in one snug layer.
5. **Loosen the mash.** Beat the cold mash with the yogurt and egg yolk until spreadable, then fold in half the cheese.
6. **Assemble.** Spoon the mash over the meatballs and rough up the top with a fork. Scatter over the remaining cheese and drizzle with the Aleppo butter.
7. **Bake.** Bake for 20–25 minutes, until the top is golden and the edges are bubbling. Rest for 10 minutes, then dust with sumac.

---

## Notes
- **Seasoned meatballs:** if the meatballs are already seasoned, halve the cumin and hold the salt until the end of step 3.
- **Sauce thickness:** whole meatballs do not thicken the sauce the way crumbled meat does, so reduce it well before assembling or the mash will sink.
- **Serving:** serve with Turkish salad or plain yogurt.
