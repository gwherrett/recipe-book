# Collard Greens with Ham Hocks (Braised)

**Source:** [Serious Eats — Daniel Gritzer](https://www.seriouseats.com/collard-greens-ham-hocks-recipe)

**Prep Time:** 5 minutes
**Cook Time:** 3 hours 35 minutes
**Yield:** 4 servings

A classic pot of braised collards with deep, smoky pork flavor and meltingly tender greens. Smoked ham hocks are simmered in chicken stock with onions until the meat falls off the bone, creating a rich pot likker that the collards then stew in — sip it like broth or sop it up with cornbread.

> *Halved from the original 8-serving recipe.*

---

## Ingredients

- ¾ pound (340 g) meaty smoked ham hocks (see notes)
- 1 medium yellow onion (about ½ pound; 225 g), sliced into 2-inch lengths
- 2 medium cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 quart (950 mL) homemade chicken stock, low-sodium store-bought chicken broth, or water
- 1½ pounds (680 g) collard greens, woody stems trimmed and leaves cut into thick ribbons
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Apple cider vinegar, to taste (optional)

**Special Equipment:** Dutch oven

---

## Directions

1. **Simmer the ham hocks:** In a large pot or Dutch oven, combine ham hocks, onion, garlic, and chicken stock and bring to a simmer. Cover and cook at a bare simmer until hocks are very tender, 2 to 3 hours.

2. **Pick the meat:** Remove ham hocks from liquid, transfer to a cutting board, and pull bones from meaty and fatty parts. Discard bones. Chop up meat into chunks and return it to pot.

3. **Braise the collards:** Add collard greens, pressing down to submerge in liquid. Return to a simmer and cook, uncovered, until collards are very tender, about 30 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Add vinegar to taste, if desired, then serve. (You can add vinegar to the pot, or let individual diners season their greens with it at the table.)

---

## Notes

- You can swap out the ham hocks for other smoked or cured pork products, like slab bacon or salt pork, as long as they aren't lean meats, like smoked pork loin. Lean meat will dry up and toughen with extended cooking.
