# Beef, Chorizo, Cheese & Basil Lasagna

**Tags:** Italian, Comfort Food, Make-Ahead, Crowd-Friendly  
**Source:** Adapted from [Sausage, Cheese & Basil Lasagna](sausage-cheese-and-basil-lasagna.md) (Epicurious)  

## Summary
A quicker lasagna with ground beef and chorizo instead of Italian sausage, layered with fresh basil, ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan. The meat is browned in a separate skillet so its fat and liquid can be drained before it joins the sauce.

## Ingredients
- 1/2 lb ground beef (85/15)  
- 1/2 lb fresh chorizo sausage, casings removed  
- 1 large onion, chopped  
- 3 cloves garlic, minced  
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes  
- 2 tbsp tomato paste  
- 2 tbsp fresh basil, chopped (plus extra for garnish)  
- 1 tsp dried oregano  
- 1 lb ricotta cheese  
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese  
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese  
- 1 large egg  
- 9 lasagna noodles (cooked al dente)  
- Olive oil  
- Salt & pepper, to taste  

## Method
1. **Brown the Meat (separate skillet):** Heat a little olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add beef and chorizo; cook, breaking up, until browned and the chorizo has rendered its fat, 7–10 minutes. Tip into a strainer over a bowl and drain. Discard the fat (keep a spoonful if you want more chorizo flavour).  
2. **Make the Sofrito & Sauce:** Meanwhile, heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add onion and garlic; cook until soft, about 8 minutes. Stir in tomato paste and cook 1 minute. Add tomatoes, drained meat, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Simmer 15–20 minutes.  
3. **Prepare Cheese Mixture:** In a bowl, combine ricotta, egg, and half the Parmesan. Season lightly.  
4. **Assemble:** Spread a thin layer of sauce in a 9x13-inch baking dish. Layer noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella, and sauce. Repeat twice, ending with sauce and remaining cheeses on top.  
5. **Bake:** Cover with foil; bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Uncover and bake another 15–20 minutes until bubbly and golden.  
6. **Rest & Serve:** Let stand 10 minutes before slicing. Garnish with fresh basil.

## Notes
- Use fresh (raw) Mexican-style chorizo so it crumbles into the sauce. If using cured Spanish chorizo, dice it finely and add it to the sofrito for the last few minutes.  
- Can be made ahead and refrigerated up to 1 day before baking.  
- Freezes well (uncooked or cooked).
