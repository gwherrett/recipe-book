# Aloo Gajar Matar (Indian Potatoes, Carrots & Peas)

**Source:** [Piping Pot Curry](https://pipingpotcurry.com/aloo-gajar-matar/)

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Yield:** 4 servings

A comforting Punjabi stir-fry of potatoes, carrots, and peas, seasoned with cumin, chili, and a tangy finish of pomegranate powder. Simple, vegan, and full of flavour.

---

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups potatoes, cubed
- 4½ cups carrots, cut into small pieces
- 1 cup green peas, fresh or frozen
- 2½ tablespoon oil
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
- 1 green chili pepper, chopped (optional)
- 2 teaspoon garlic, minced
- ¼ teaspoon ground turmeric (haldi powder)
- ½ teaspoon red chili powder (mirchi powder), adjust to taste
- 1 teaspoon salt, adjust to taste
- 3 tablespoon water
- 1 teaspoon pomegranate powder (anardana), or dry mango powder (amchur)*

---

## Instructions

1. **Prep vegetables:** Peel and cut the potatoes and carrots.

2. **Bloom spices:** Heat oil in a large wok or kadhai on high flame. Add cumin seeds, green chili, and garlic and sauté for 30 seconds. Add turmeric, red chili powder, and salt.

3. **Add potato and peas:** Add the cubed potato and stir well. If using fresh green peas, add them now.

4. **Add carrots:** Add carrots and water and stir them in.

5. **Cook through:** Cover with lid and cook on medium flame for 15 minutes, stirring at regular intervals. If using frozen green peas, add them at about 10 minutes.

6. **Check doneness:** Check that the vegetables are cooked by pressing with a ladle.

7. **Finish:** Add pomegranate powder and mix well. Check seasoning and adjust if needed.

8. **Serve:** Garnish with cilantro and enjoy with roti or paratha.

---

## Notes

- Cooking time can vary depending on the size of the diced carrots and potatoes. In this recipe they are cut into very small pieces.
- If you don't have pomegranate powder or dry mango powder on hand, substitute with lime or lemon juice.
- You can use garlic paste instead of minced garlic.
- To add more depth, sauté onions and tomatoes in the oil before adding the other vegetables.
