# All-Day Meat Lasagna

## Ingredients

### Ragù Bolognese
- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 2/3 pound ground lamb (or 85/15 ground beef)
- 2/3 pound ground pork
- 2/3 pound ground veal
- 4 ounces chicken livers, finely chopped (optional)
- 1 large onion, finely chopped (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 2 large carrots, peeled and cut into 1/4-inch dice (about 1 cup)
- 3 large ribs celery, peeled and cut into 1/4-inch dice (about 1 cup)
- 4 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 1/2 cup fresh sage leaves, finely chopped
- Large pinch red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 (28-ounce) can crushed Italian plum tomatoes (preferably D.O.P. San Marzano)
- 1 1/2 cups dry red wine
- 1 1/2 cups whole milk
- 2 cups chicken or veal stock (or low-sodium canned chicken broth)
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 cup minced basil or parsley (or a mix)

### Ricotta Mixture
- 3 cups whole-milk ricotta
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 2 large eggs
- 1/4 cup minced basil or parsley (or a mix)

### Besciamella
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 2 cups whole milk
- 1/2 pound low-moisture whole-milk mozzarella, grated
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

### Assembly
- 15 sheets fresh pasta (4×8 inches) or 15 no-boil lasagna noodles
- 4 ounces Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated (about 2 cups)
- 2 tablespoons minced basil or parsley (or a mix)

## Instructions

### Ragù Bolognese
1. Heat butter and olive oil in a large Dutch oven over high heat until butter stops foaming. Add lamb, pork, veal, and chicken livers; cook, breaking up meat, until no longer pink, 7–10 minutes.
2. Remove from heat, transfer meat to a strainer set over a bowl and drain. Return drained liquid to the Dutch oven.
3. Add onions, carrots, celery, garlic, sage, and red pepper flakes. Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until vegetables are softened but not browned, about 10 minutes.
4. Return meat to pan. Add tomatoes, wine, milk, stock, and bay leaves; bring to a simmer over high heat. Reduce heat to low, partially cover, and simmer 2 1/2–3 hours, stirring occasionally, until liquid is just below the level of the meat (about 2 quarts of sauce). Add extra stock if needed.
5. Remove bay leaves. Add fish sauce and heavy cream; simmer until fat is emulsified, about 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper. Remove from heat and rest 30 minutes. Stir in herbs. (Bolognese keeps up to 1 week in the fridge and improves with time; reheat before using.)

### Ricotta Mixture
6. Process or whisk ricotta until smooth. Season with salt and pepper. Add eggs and herbs; mix until incorporated. Set aside.

### Besciamella
7. Melt butter in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat until foaming subsides, about 1 minute. Add flour and whisk until light blond and slightly nutty, about 1 minute. Add garlic and stir to combine.
8. Whisking constantly, pour in milk in a steady stream until fully incorporated. Bring to a simmer until thickened. Remove from heat, add mozzarella and nutmeg, and whisk until melted. Return to a simmer briefly, then remove from heat and season with salt and pepper.

### Assembly
9. If using no-boil noodles, soak in a baking dish with hot water for 10 minutes, changing water once. Drain and pat dry on kitchen towels.
10. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) with racks on the lower-middle and lowest positions.
11. Spread 1/6 of the ragù (about 1 1/3 cups) on the bottom of a 13×9-inch baking dish; drizzle with 1/2 cup besciamella. Layer 3 pasta sheets on top.
12. **Layer 2:** 1/6 ragù, 1/2 cup besciamella, 1/3 cup Parmesan, pasta.
13. **Layer 3:** 1/2 of ricotta mixture, 1/6 ragù, 1/3 cup Parmesan, pasta.
14. **Layer 4:** 1/6 ragù, 1/2 cup besciamella, 1/3 cup Parmesan, pasta.
15. **Layer 5:** remaining ricotta mixture, 1/6 ragù, 1/3 cup Parmesan, pasta.
16. **Top:** remaining ragù, remaining besciamella (about 1 cup), remaining 2/3 cup Parmesan.
17. Place a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet on the lower rack to catch drips. Bake lasagna on the upper rack until edges are crisping and top is bubbly and golden brown, about 45 minutes, rotating halfway. Rest 10 minutes, sprinkle with herbs, and serve.

## Notes
- For the best ricotta, make fresh or use a brand with no gums or stabilizers (milk, salt, and acid/starter only).
- A layer of fat may form on top of the ragù during its long simmer — do not skim it off; it emulsifies in at the end.
- The ragù can be made up to a week ahead and refrigerated; it improves with time.
