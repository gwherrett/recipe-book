# Shrimp Okra Gumbo

**Source:** [Louisiana Woman Blog](https://louisianawomanblog.com/2022/09/30/shrimp-okra-gumbo/)

**Prep Time:** 20 minutes
**Cook Time:** 1 hour 45 minutes
**Yield:** 6 servings

A rich-tasting shrimp gumbo seasoned and thickened with smothered okra and vegetables.

---

## Ingredients

### Smothered Okra

- 1 tablespoon canola or vegetable oil
- 4 cups fresh or frozen sliced okra, or 16 ounces
- ½ cup chopped onion
- ¼ cup chopped celery
- ¼ cup chopped bell pepper
- 1 cup chopped fresh or canned tomatoes
- 2 garlic pods, pressed
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon red cayenne pepper

### Gumbo

- 2 cups smothered okra from the above recipe
- 2 pounds fresh shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 4 cups (32 ounces) chicken broth
- 1 cup water
- Kitchen Bouquet, optional
- Fresh green onion and/or parsley to garnish, chopped

---

## Instructions

### Smothered Okra

1. **Heat the oil:** Place a heavy pot on a burner and heat the oil.

2. **Cook the okra:** Add the okra to the pot and cook on medium-low heat, stirring occasionally and being careful not to burn the okra.

3. **Brown the okra:** Cook until the okra is browned and no longer slimy, which takes about 30 minutes to 1 hour; add a small amount of water if the okra sticks to the bottom of the pot.

4. **Add the vegetables:** Add onions, bell peppers, celery, and tomatoes, and cook for 20 minutes or more until the vegetables are soft and browned some more; again, add a small amount of water if the vegetables begin to stick to the pot.

5. **Add the garlic:** Stir in the garlic and let it simmer for a few more minutes.

6. **Season:** Season with salt and red cayenne pepper.

### Gumbo

1. **Cook the shrimp:** Stir the shrimp into the smothered okra, then simmer, stirring often, for 5 minutes on medium-low heat until the shrimp is cooked.

2. **Add the liquid:** Add chicken broth and water, and simmer on low heat for 20 minutes.

3. **Adjust thickness:** Pour small amounts of water if needed to adjust the thickness of the gumbo.

4. **Adjust seasoning and colour:** Taste to adjust seasoning and add Kitchen Bouquet as a browning agent if the gumbo is not brown enough.

5. **Serve:** Serve hot gumbo in a bowl ladled over cooked rice.

---

## Notes

- The more okra you cook with, the longer it takes to eliminate the slime.
- Smother the okra ahead of time and refrigerate or freeze it to save time on gumbo prep.
- Before adding them to the okra, pass your hand through the shrimp to ensure they are free of shells and black veins.
- Be sure to use a small amount of Kitchen Bouquet; it only takes a dab to brown, and you don't want to use too much, or it will alter the gumbo flavor.
- Season the gumbo with Cajun seasoning at the end of cooking.
