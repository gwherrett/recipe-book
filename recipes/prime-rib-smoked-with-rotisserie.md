# Texas-Style Rotisserie Smoked Prime Rib (2–3 Ribs)
*Gas BBQ + Smoker Box + Mustard Binder*

## 🧄 Ingredients

### Binder:
- 2 tbsp yellow mustard

### Dry Rub:
- 1½ tbsp kosher salt  
- 1½ tbsp coarse black pepper  
- 2 tsp garlic powder  
- 2 tsp onion powder  
- ½ tsp smoked paprika *(optional)*  
- 2 tsp finely minced **fresh rosemary**  
- 2 tsp finely minced **fresh thyme**

### Baste (Optional):
- ⅓ cup beef broth  
- 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar  
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce  
- 2 tsp oil or melted beef tallow  
- ½ tsp rub mix  
- Optional: splash of beer or brewed coffee

---

## 🔧 Setup

- **Grill:** Gas BBQ with one burner on for indirect heat  
- **Smoker Box:** Filled with oak, post oak, or hickory chips  
- **Drip Pan:** Under rotisserie  
- **Rotisserie:** Skewer and secure roast carefully  
- **Target Temp:** 250–275°F (121–135°C)

---

## 🔪 Instructions

1. **Prep:**
   - Let roast rest at room temp for 1 hour.
   - Pat dry, coat with mustard, and apply rub evenly.
   - Skewer roast on rotisserie spit and secure tightly.

2. **Grill Setup:**
   - Preheat one burner to medium-low.
   - Load smoker box and place drip pan under roast area.

3. **Cook:**
   - Mount spit and start rotisserie.
   - Close lid and monitor internal grill temp.
   - Add fresh wood chips as needed for smoke.

4. **Baste (optional):**
   - Apply lightly every 45–60 minutes using a brush or spritz bottle.

5. **Doneness:**
   - Pull roast when internal temp hits:
     - 120°F for rare  
     - 130°F for medium-rare  
     - 135°F for medium  
   - Rest 30 minutes under foil before carving.

6. **Serve:**
   - Remove bones if desired and slice thick.

---

## ⏱️ Estimated Cook Time for 5 lbs

| Doneness       | Internal Temp | Pull Temp | Estimated Cook Time |
|----------------|----------------|------------|----------------------|
| Rare           | 125°F / 52°C   | 120°F      | ~3 hours             |
| Medium-Rare ✅ | 130°F / 54°C   | 125–127°F  | ~3 to 3¼ hours       |
| Medium         | 135°F / 57°C   | 130°F      | ~3½ hours            |

> ✅ Medium-rare is ideal for smoked prime rib. Start checking temp at 2.5 hours. Rest 30 minutes before slicing.
