# Sage-Oil Sous Vide Turkey Drumsticks, Pulled, with Cider-Maple Sauce

Cured, cooked sous vide in sage oil until falling apart, charred on the grill, then pulled and tossed in an autumn pan sauce.

---

## Ingredients

### Cure

- 3 turkey drumsticks (about 2 kg total)
- 30 grams kosher salt (about 1.5% of meat weight)
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 4 garlic cloves, grated
- 3 tablespoons fresh sage, finely chopped
- 1 orange, zested

### Sous vide

- 6 tablespoons sage oil (for the bags)
- 3 fresh sage sprigs

### Cider-maple sauce

- 1 shallot, minced
- 250 milliliters unfiltered apple cider (or apple juice)
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 2 tablespoons cold butter, divided

### Fried sage

- 12 whole sage leaves, for frying
- 3 tablespoons sage oil (for frying)

---

## Instructions

1. **Cure:** Mix the kosher salt, black pepper, grated garlic, chopped sage and orange zest. Rub it all over the turkey drumsticks, working some under the skin. Set the drumsticks on a rack over a tray and refrigerate uncovered for 12–24 hours.

2. **Bag:** Brush off the loose cure without rinsing. Bag each drumstick with 2 tbsp of the sage oil (for the bags) and one of the fresh sage sprigs. Wrap the bony knuckle ends in plastic wrap or double-bag them so they don't puncture the bags. Seal.

3. **Sous vide:** Cook at 80°C for 12 hours, until the meat pulls off the bone. Cover the bath (plastic wrap or a lid) to limit evaporation, and weigh the bags down so they stay submerged.

4. **Chill:** Move the bags to an ice bath for 30 minutes, then refrigerate them until you're ready to grill (up to 3 days).

5. **Unbag and dry:** Open the bags, pour all the juices and oil into a jug, and refrigerate them. Pat the drumsticks very dry and leave them on a rack for about 20 minutes while the grill heats.

6. **Make the sauce:** Skim most of the fat off the reserved juices. Sweat the shallot in half of the cold butter, then add the skimmed juices, apple cider, apple cider vinegar and maple syrup. Simmer until reduced by about two-thirds and syrupy. Off the heat, whisk in the Dijon mustard and the rest of the butter.

7. **Grill:** Set up two zones: one side on medium-high, the other off. Warm the drumsticks on the cool side with the lid closed for 15 minutes. Then move them over direct heat and turn them until the skin blisters and chars, about 5–8 minutes. Watch for flare-ups and shift the legs to the cool side if they happen.

8. **Fry the sage:** Heat the sage oil (for frying) in a small pan and fry the whole sage leaves for about 30 seconds, until crisp. Drain on paper towel and salt lightly.

9. **Pull and dress:** Once the drumsticks are cool enough to handle, pull the meat into chunks. Pick out all the needle-like tendons carefully. Chop the crisp skin and toss it with the meat and enough sauce to coat. Top with the fried sage and serve the remaining sauce on the side.

---

## Notes

- Turkey drumsticks contain a dozen or so thin, bony tendons. They pull out easily at this texture but are unpleasant to bite into, so be thorough.
- Serving ideas: brioche buns with an apple-fennel slaw, or over roasted squash or soft polenta.
