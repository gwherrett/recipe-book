# Panzanella (Tuscan Bread Salad)

**Category:** Salads & Bowls  
**Tags:** Italian, vegetarian, summer, bread, tomatoes, basil

## Summary

A classic Tuscan salad of ripe tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and basil tossed with toasted bread cubes in a tangy olive oil and vinegar dressing. The tomatoes are salted first so their juices soak into the bread along with the dressing. Perfect for using up stale bread and celebrating peak summer produce.

---

## Ingredients

- 4 cups **stale crusty bread** (Tuscan or rustic country loaf), cut or torn into 1-inch cubes
- 3 large **ripe tomatoes**, cut into chunks
- 1 **cucumber**, sliced
- 1 **red onion**, thinly sliced
- 1 small **clove garlic**, finely minced *(optional)*
- 1 bunch **fresh basil**, torn
- 1/2 cup **extra-virgin olive oil** *(use a good one)*
- 3 tbsp **red wine vinegar** *(or white wine vinegar)*
- **Salt and freshly ground black pepper**, to taste
- *(Optional)* 1/2 cup mozzarella or feta cheese, cubed

---

## Method

1. **Toast the bread:** Spread the bread cubes on a baking sheet and toast at 375°F (190°C) for ~10 minutes, until crisp but not hard. Even stale bread benefits from a quick toast.
2. **Draw out the tomato juices:** In a large bowl, combine tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and garlic (if using). Season with a pinch of salt and let sit 10–15 minutes.
3. **Make the dressing:** Whisk olive oil, vinegar, salt, and pepper until emulsified.
4. **Assemble:** Add the bread and basil to the vegetables, pour over the dressing, and toss well so the bread picks up the tomato juices. Add cheese if using.
5. **Rest & serve:** Let sit at room temperature for at least 30 minutes (up to 2 hours) so the bread absorbs the flavours. Adjust seasoning and serve at room temperature.

---

## Notes

- For a more traditional Tuscan version, skip the cheese and keep it to bread, tomatoes, and olive oil.
- Add capers or olives for a briny twist.
- For a heartier version, include grilled chicken or roasted peppers.
- Best eaten within a few hours of mixing.
