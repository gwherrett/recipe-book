# Pla Neung Manao (Steamed Fish with Lime & Garlic)

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes
**Yield:** 2–3 servings

Whole fish or fillets steamed over lemongrass and dressed with a hot, sour garlic-chili-lime sauce. Oil-free and rich in omega-3s, about 200–250 kcal per serving.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 whole white fish (sea bass, tilapia or snapper, about 700 g), or 2–3 fillets (halibut or cod work well)
- 2 lemongrass stalks, bruised
- Bok choy or Chinese broccoli, to steam alongside

### Sauce

- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 4–6 Thai chilies, finely chopped
- 4 tbsp lime juice
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp honey or palm sugar
- ½ cup low-sodium chicken stock

### Garnish

- Cilantro
- Lime slices

---

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the fish:** Score the fish and place the lemongrass inside or underneath it on a heatproof plate.

2. **Steam:** Steam 12–15 minutes for a whole fish or 8–10 minutes for fillets, until it flakes easily. Add the greens around the fish for the last 4 minutes.

3. **Make the sauce:** Meanwhile, warm the stock and stir in the garlic, chilies, lime juice, fish sauce and honey.

4. **Serve:** Pour the hot sauce over the fish and garnish with cilantro and lime slices.
