# Spiced Rye

A rye whisky take on the spiced rum infusion. Warm spices, orange, and ginger, with tart dried barberries standing in for the fruity edge rum gets from molasses. No peppercorns, since rye is peppery enough on its own.

## Ingredients

- 1 bottle high-proof rye (750 ml, as high as you can find, ideally 50%+)
- 1/2 bottle good aged rye
- 1 vanilla bean, split lengthwise
- 3 whole cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick, broken into pieces
- 5 whole allspice berries
- 1/2 piece star anise (watch amount, quite strong)
- 1/8 tsp fresh-grated nutmeg
- 3 pieces fresh ginger slices
- 3-4 strips fresh orange zest (3-inch each, white pith removed)
- 2 tbsp dried barberries, rinsed (optional, adds tart fruit)

## Method

1. Combine all ingredients except the aged rye in a large jar and seal.
2. Store in a cool, dark place for 2–4 days, shaking once daily to redistribute the ingredients.
3. Begin tasting after 48 hours. Adjust spices if needed.
4. Once the flavor is where you want it (usually not more than 4 days), strain through a fine mesh sieve.
5. Add to mini barrel. Top up with aged rye and water to bring alcohol down.

## Notes

- Rye has no molasses sweetness. For a rounder profile, add 1–2 tsp demerara, turbinado, or maple syrup.
- Barberries are tart like cranberries, so they pair well with orange. Start with 2 tbsp; use fewer if you want the spice to lead.
- Clove and allspice hit harder against rye than rum. Taste from day 2 and pull them early if needed.
- Peppercorns left out on purpose. Rye already supplies the pepper.
- Good in Old Fashioned and Manhattan variations, or warm with apple cider.
