# 🧀 Blue Cheese Dressing

**Source:** Adapted from [Nigella Lawson's Universally Useful Blue Cheese Dressing](https://www.nigella.com/recipes/universally-useful-blue-cheese-dressing) (*Kitchen*, 2010)

**Yield:** about 250 ml / 1 cup

A thick, tangy blue cheese dressing built on buttermilk rather than mayo or sour cream. Most of the cheese is blended in for a smooth, cheese-forward base, and the rest is crumbled in at the end so there are still chunks to bite into. Useful for salads, dipping, and as a sauce for roast beef.

---

## Ingredients

- 150 g blue cheese (e.g., Stilton, Gorgonzola, or Roquefort), divided:
  - 100 g for blending
  - 50 g, crumbled, to finish
- 75 ml buttermilk (or runny plain yogurt)
- 45 ml full-fat milk
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp brown sauce (such as A1 or HP)
- 1 tsp raw apple cider vinegar (or good white wine vinegar)
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### Optional

- 1 tbsp finely chopped chives or parsley, for a fresh finish

---

## Method

1. **Blend the base:** Put 100 g of the blue cheese, the buttermilk, milk, Worcestershire sauce, brown sauce, and vinegar into a blender (or a jug for a stick blender). Blitz until smooth and thick.
2. **Adjust:** Taste and add black pepper. If it's too thick to pour, loosen with a splash more milk.
3. **Crumble in the rest:** Transfer to a bowl or jar and gently stir in the remaining 50 g of crumbled blue cheese (and herbs, if using), leaving visible chunks.
4. **Chill:** Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes to let the flavours meld. Keeps up to 2 days in the fridge.

---

## Serving Suggestions

- **Wedge salad:** Cut a head of iceberg lettuce into wedges, dollop with the dressing, and top with crisp bacon, cherry tomatoes, thin-sliced red onion or chives, and cracked black pepper.
- Dribble over sliced sweet tomatoes with a torn-off length of baguette for an easy supper.
- Use as a sauce for leftover rare roast beef.
- Serve with baked potatoes.
- Eat as a dip with chips or crudités.
