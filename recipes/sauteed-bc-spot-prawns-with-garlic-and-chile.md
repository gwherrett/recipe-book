# Sautéed BC Spot Prawns with Garlic and Chile

**Prep Time:** 10 minutes
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Yield:** 2–4 servings

Fresh BC spot prawns sautéed in olive oil with garlic, chile, and lemon — quick, bright, and best enjoyed family style.

---

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup olive oil
- 1 to 2 pounds BC spot prawns
- Salt
- 4 to 6 cloves garlic, roughly chopped or sliced thin
- 1 or 2 small hot chiles, sliced thin, or 2 dried hot chiles, crumbled
- Juice of 1 lemon

---

## Instructions

1. **Thaw prawns:** Thaw your frozen BC spot prawns and pat dry.

2. **Season prawns:** Season the prawns with salt.

3. **Sauté garlic and chile:** Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and chiles and cook until fragrant, about 1–2 minutes.

4. **Cook prawns:** Add the prawns to the pan and sauté, turning once, until pink and just cooked through, about 2 minutes per side.

5. **Serve:** Squeeze the lemon juice over the prawns and serve immediately.

---

## Notes

- Best enjoyed family style.
- The prawns can be served on top of a steak or a salad.
- The prawns need to be peeled before eating.
