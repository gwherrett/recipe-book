# Moros y Cristianos (Cuban Black Beans and Rice)

**Source:** Traditional Cuban method, written up from the standard technique (not adapted from a single published recipe)

**Yield:** 4–6 servings

**Time:** About 35 minutes with canned beans

A classic Cuban side of white rice cooked together with black beans. The name ("Moors and Christians") is a nod to the black beans and white rice. Built on a green pepper sofrito and finished with a splash of vinegar, it is the traditional partner for Ropa Vieja.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 1½ cups cooked black beans (about 1 can, drained and rinsed), plus their liquid or water (see below)
- 2 cups liquid: reserved bean cooking liquid, topped up with water or low-sodium broth
- 1 tbsp white or red wine vinegar
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped, for garnish (optional)

---

## Instructions

1. **Make the sofrito:** Heat the olive oil in a medium pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion and green pepper and cook until soft, 5–7 minutes.

2. **Add aromatics and spices:** Stir in the garlic, cumin and oregano and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

3. **Toast the rice:** Add the rice and stir for 1 minute so the grains are coated in the oil.

4. **Combine:** Add the black beans, the 2 cups of liquid, the bay leaves and a good pinch of salt. Bring to a boil.

5. **Cook the rice:** Reduce the heat to low, cover tightly, and cook without lifting the lid for 18–20 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed and the rice is tender.

6. **Rest and finish:** Take the pot off the heat and leave it covered for 5 minutes. Remove the bay leaves, fluff with a fork, and stir in the vinegar. Adjust the salt and pepper and garnish with cilantro or parsley.

---

## Notes

- **Dried beans:** Soak 1 cup dried black beans overnight, then simmer in fresh water for 1–1½ hours until tender. Use the cooking liquid for the rice, since it gives the rice its dark colour and flavour.
- **Salt:** If you use canned beans and the liquid from the can, go easy on the salt. Pair with Ropa Vieja, which already has olives and capers.
- **Congrí variation:** The Eastern Cuban version of this dish is traditionally cooked with pork or bacon for extra richness.
- **Serving:** Goes well with Ropa Vieja, fried plantains, or Yuca con Mojo.
- Leftovers keep 3–4 days in the fridge.
