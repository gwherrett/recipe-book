# Turkey Chili (Guajillo & Smoked Paprika)

**Category:** Mains → Chili & Stews  
**Tags:** Ground turkey, guajillo chili, slow cooker, chili, freezer-friendly, rich & smoky  
**Yield:** ~8–10 servings (3.5–4 L)

---

### Ingredients
- 1.6 kg ground turkey (dark and light meat mix preferred)  
- 3 tbsp olive oil  
- 2 large onions, chopped  
- 4 celery stalks, chopped  
- 2 red bell peppers, chopped  
- 6 cloves garlic, minced  
- 4 tbsp tomato paste  
- 2 × 400 g cans crushed tomatoes  
- 2½ cups broth (chicken or vegetable)  
- 2 × 400 g cans kidney or black beans *(optional)*  
- 2 tbsp chili powder  
- 3–4 dried guajillo chiles *(seeded, toasted, and blended — see below)*  
- 2 tsp smoked paprika  
- 2 tsp ground cumin  
- 1 tsp ground coriander  
- 1 tsp cinnamon  
- 1 tsp dried oregano  
- ½ tsp cayenne (or to taste)  
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar *or* juice of 1 lime  
- Salt and black pepper, to taste  
- 2 tsp cocoa powder *or* 2 tsp harissa paste *(optional, for extra depth)*  

---

### 🔥 Guajillo Chili Paste
1. Remove stems and seeds from guajillos.  
2. Toast lightly in a dry skillet (30–60 seconds per side, until fragrant).  
3. Cover with hot water and soak 15–20 minutes until softened.  
4. Blend with ½ cup soaking liquid and 1 clove garlic into a smooth paste.  
5. Add during the “Combine” step below.

---

### Method

1. **Brown the Turkey**  
   Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high. Brown turkey in batches until no longer pink. Season with salt and pepper.

2. **Sauté Aromatics**  
   In the same pan, sauté onions, celery, and peppers for 5–7 minutes. Add garlic and tomato paste; cook 1 minute to caramelize.

3. **Combine in Slow Cooker**  
   Transfer all ingredients to a 6–8 qt slow cooker: browned turkey, aromatics, crushed tomatoes, broth, guajillo paste, beans (if using), spices, vinegar/lime, and cocoa/harissa (if using). Stir thoroughly.

4. **Cook Low & Slow**  
   - Low: 7–8 hours  
   - High: 4–5 hours  
   Stir once during cooking if convenient.

5. **Finish**  
   Taste and adjust salt, spice, and acidity. If too loose, cook uncovered on High for 20–30 minutes to thicken.

6. **Serve**  
   Garnish with cilantro, diced onion, or a dollop of yogurt or sour cream. Serve with rice, cornbread, or flatbread.

---

### ✨ Notes
- Guajillo adds earthy, dried-fruit depth and mild heat.  
- Optional cocoa or harissa gives smoky sweetness and richness.  
- Freezes beautifully; reheat gently with a splash of broth or water.
