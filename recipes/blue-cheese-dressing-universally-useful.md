# Blue Cheese Dressing (Universally Useful)

**Source:** [Nigella.com](https://www.nigella.com/recipes/universally-useful-blue-cheese-dressing) (*Kitchen*, 2010)

**Yield:** 250ml / 1 cup

A thick, tangy blue cheese dressing from Nigella Lawson's *Kitchen*, useful for salads, dipping, and as a sauce for roast beef.

---

## Ingredients

- 150 grams blue cheese (crumbled)
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon brown sauce (such as A1 or HP)
- 75 millilitres buttermilk or runny plain yogurt
- 45 millilitres full fat milk
- 1 teaspoon raw apple cider vinegar (or good white wine vinegar)

---

## Serving Suggestions

- Dribble over sliced, sweet tomatoes with a torn-off log of baguette for a perfect enough supper.
- For a retro US-steakhouse-style starter, cut a head of iceberg lettuce into wedges, dollop each crunchy wedge with the dressing, and scatter with crisp-fried bacon crumbles.
- Use as a sauce for leftover rare roast beef.
- Serve with baked potatoes.
- Eat as a dip with chips or crudités.
