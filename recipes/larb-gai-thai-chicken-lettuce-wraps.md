# Larb Gai (Thai Chicken Lettuce Wraps)

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 10 minutes
**Yield:** 4 servings

Lean ground chicken tossed with lime, fish sauce, fresh herbs and toasted rice powder, served in lettuce cups. High protein, low carb and cooked without oil, about 250 kcal per serving.

---

## Ingredients

- 500 g lean ground chicken or turkey
- 2 tbsp toasted rice powder (dry-toast uncooked rice, then grind)
- 3 shallots, thinly sliced
- 3 green onions, chopped
- ½ cup mint, chopped
- ½ cup cilantro, chopped
- 3 tbsp lime juice
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp chili flakes

### To serve

- Butter lettuce leaves
- Cucumber
- Green beans

---

## Instructions

1. **Cook the chicken:** Cook the chicken in a dry pan with 3–4 tbsp water, breaking it up, for 6–8 minutes until cooked through.

2. **Dress:** Remove from the heat and mix in the lime juice, fish sauce and chili flakes.

3. **Fold in the aromatics:** Fold in the shallots, green onions, mint, cilantro and toasted rice powder.

4. **Serve:** Spoon into lettuce cups and serve with cucumber and green beans.
