# Ratatouille with Celery & Corn

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 35–45 minutes
**Yield:** 4–6 servings

A Provençal-inspired ratatouille without eggplant, built around zucchini, peppers, celery, sweet corn and ripe tomatoes. Celery adds aromatic crunch and corn brings gentle sweetness.

---

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil, plus more to finish
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2–3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, seeded and chopped
- 1 yellow bell pepper, seeded and chopped
- 3–4 celery stalks, sliced ½ inch thick (about 2 cups)
- 2 medium zucchinis, cut into ½–1 inch half-moons (about 600 g)
- 2–3 ripe tomatoes, chopped (or 1 × 400 g can diced tomatoes, drained)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp dried thyme (or a few fresh sprigs)
- 1 tsp dried rosemary or 2–3 fresh sprigs (optional)
- 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Kernels from 2 ears fresh corn (about 2 cups) or 2 cups frozen corn (no need to thaw)
- 1–2 tsp red wine vinegar or lemon juice
- Handful fresh basil or parsley, torn

---

## Method

1. **Start the base:** Heat the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook 4–5 minutes until translucent. Stir in the garlic and cook 30–60 seconds until fragrant.

2. **Build the vegetables:** Add the bell peppers and celery and cook 5–6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they start to soften.

3. **Add the zucchini:** Stir in the zucchini and cook 5–7 minutes, until lightly golden in spots.

4. **Simmer:** Add the tomatoes, bay leaf, thyme, rosemary (if using), 1 tsp salt and several grinds of pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover and cook 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.

5. **Finish with corn:** Uncover, stir in the corn and simmer 5–10 minutes more, until the corn is just tender and the stew has thickened. Remove the bay leaf and any rosemary sprigs.

6. **Balance and serve:** Off the heat, adjust the salt and pepper and brighten with a splash of red wine vinegar or lemon juice. Finish with a drizzle of olive oil and the torn basil or parsley.

---

## Notes

- **Texture:** Aim for tender but distinct vegetables, not mush. For a looser, saucier ratatouille, add ¼ cup water or vegetable stock while it simmers; for a thicker one, cook uncovered a few minutes longer.
- **Corn timing:** Adding the corn at the end keeps it sweet and crisp. For a softer, more melded stew, add it with the zucchini instead.
- **Make-ahead:** Tastes even better the next day. Keeps 4 days refrigerated. Reheat gently, adding a splash of water if needed.
- **Serve with:** Crusty bread, creamy polenta, couscous or rice, or as a topping for grilled fish or chicken.
- **Protein ideas:** Top with crumbled feta or goat cheese, or add a can of chickpeas during the simmer.
- **Substitutions:** Swap the basil for mint. Add olives or capers for briny depth. A pat of butter at the end adds gloss.

## Variations

- **Spicy:** Add ½ tsp chili flakes with the garlic.
- **Smoky:** Stir in ½ tsp smoked paprika with the tomatoes.
- **Herby:** Swap the thyme and rosemary for 1 tsp herbes de Provence.
