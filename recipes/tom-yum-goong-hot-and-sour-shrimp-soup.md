# Tom Yum Goong (Hot & Sour Shrimp Soup)

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 20 minutes
**Yield:** 4 servings

A clear, broth-based hot and sour soup fragrant with lemongrass, galangal and lime leaves. Almost no fat, about 150–200 kcal per serving.

---

## Ingredients

- 6 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable stock
- 3 lemongrass stalks, cut into 2" pieces and bruised
- 5 slices galangal (or ginger)
- 6 kaffir lime leaves, torn
- 3–5 Thai chilies, smashed
- 250 g mushrooms (straw or oyster), halved
- 400 g shrimp, peeled
- 2 tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 2–3 tbsp fish sauce
- 3–4 tbsp lime juice
- Cilantro, to garnish

---

## Instructions

1. **Infuse the broth:** Bring the stock to a simmer with the lemongrass, galangal, lime leaves and chilies for 10 minutes.

2. **Add the vegetables:** Add the mushrooms and tomatoes and cook 3 minutes.

3. **Cook the shrimp:** Add the shrimp and cook 2–3 minutes until pink.

4. **Season off the heat:** Remove from the heat, then stir in the fish sauce and lime juice. Taste and adjust.

5. **Serve:** Top with cilantro.

---

## Notes

- Skip the coconut milk version (tom yum nam khon) to keep it light.
- Add fish sauce gradually; it is the main source of sodium.
