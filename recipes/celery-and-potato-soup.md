# Celery and Potato Soup

**Source:** Adapted from [MasterClass Celery Soup](https://www.masterclass.com/articles/celery-soup-recipe) and Crema di Sedano (Italian cookbook)

**Prep Time:** 25 minutes
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes
**Yield:** 4 servings

This soup combines two recipes. The Italian crema di sedano adds potatoes, which make it thick and velvety without cream. The MasterClass version adds fennel seed, bay leaf and fresh dill. A spoonful of yogurt at the end gives it a bright, tangy finish.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 teaspoon fennel seeds
- 1 bunch of celery (about 3½ cups), ends trimmed (celery leaves reserved for garnish), chopped into 1-inch pieces
- 3 potatoes, diced
- 4 cups vegetable stock or chicken stock
- 1 bay leaf
- ¼ cup plain yogurt
- Kosher salt and black pepper to taste
- ½ cup chopped fresh dill, for garnish
- Croutons, for garnish

---

## Instructions

1. **Sweat the aromatics:** Heat the oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the onion and fennel seeds and cook, stirring, until the onion is soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.

2. **Add the vegetables:** Stir in the celery and potatoes and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

3. **Simmer:** Pour in the stock, add the bay leaf and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer, covered, until the potatoes and celery are very tender, 15–20 minutes.

4. **Blend:** Remove and discard the bay leaf. Purée the soup until smooth with an immersion blender or in batches in a food processor, then return it to the pot.

5. **Finish with yogurt:** Take the pot off the heat and stir in the yogurt. Season with salt and pepper.

6. **Serve:** Ladle into bowls and garnish with the dill, reserved celery leaves and croutons.

---

## Notes

- Stir the yogurt in off the heat, and don't let the soup boil afterwards, or the yogurt may curdle. If you're reheating leftovers, warm them gently.
- Greek yogurt makes the soup richer and thicker. If you use it, thin it with a spoonful of the hot soup before stirring it in.
- Use vegetable stock to keep the soup vegetarian.
