# Saag Gosht (Lamb Curry)

**Source:** Adapted from Easy Indian Cookbook (Lamb Palak) and Sanjeev Kapoor (No-Oil Saag Gosht)

**Yield:** 4 servings

An original adaptation that pressure cooks lamb offcuts in spiced broth, then skims the fat before finishing the meat in a yogurt masala with blanched spinach (or kale) paste and dill.

---

## Ingredients

### Lamb and broth

- 900 g lamb offcuts
- 2 cups broth
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 black cardamom pod
- 3 green cardamom pods
- 4 whole cloves
- 1 inch cinnamon stick
- 8 black peppercorns
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt

### Greens paste

- 500 g spinach (or kale, finely shredded)
- 2 green chilies

### Masala

- 2 tbsp reserved lamb fat (or ghee)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 6 garlic cloves, chopped
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- 1 inch ginger, julienned (divided)
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp amchur (dry mango powder)
- 1 large tomato, chopped
- 1 tsp garam masala
- ½ cup plain whole-milk yogurt

### To finish

- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp kasuri methi

---

## Method

1. **Pressure cook the lamb:** Combine the offcuts, broth, whole spices, turmeric and salt in the pressure cooker. Cook on high pressure for 35 minutes and let the pressure release naturally.

2. **Separate:** Strain, keeping both the meat and the broth, and pick out the whole spices. Skim or chill the broth to separate the fat, and reserve 2 tbsp of the lamb fat for cooking.

3. **Make the greens paste:** Blanch the greens in salted boiling water (1–2 minutes for spinach, 3–4 for kale), then plunge them into ice water to hold the colour. Squeeze them dry and blend with the green chilies to a coarse paste.

4. **Bloom the aromatics:** Heat the lamb fat on sauté. Add the cumin seeds and let them splutter, then add the garlic and cook until light golden.

5. **Brown the onions:** Add the onions and half the ginger, and cook until golden, about 10 minutes.

6. **Spices and tomato:** Stir in the chili powder, coriander and amchur for 30 seconds, adding a splash of broth if they stick. Add the tomato and cook until pulpy, about 5 minutes, then stir in the garam masala.

7. **Yogurt:** Lower the heat and stir in the yogurt a spoonful at a time so it doesn't split.

8. **Return the lamb:** Add the meat and about 1 cup of the skimmed broth, and simmer for 5 minutes.

9. **Add the greens:** Stir in the greens paste and the dill. Simmer for 5–6 minutes, loosening with more broth if needed, and adjust the salt.

10. **Finish:** Crush the kasuri methi between your palms over the pot and garnish with the remaining ginger.

---

## Notes

- If the offcuts aren't tender after 35 minutes, give them another 10 minutes under pressure.
- For deeper flavour, brown the offcuts in a little fat before pressure cooking.
- Freeze the leftover skimmed broth for another curry or soup.
- For a more pungent, traditional saag, add mustard or radish greens to the paste (Whisk Affair's approach).
- Easy Indian Cookbook tip: don't skip the kasuri methi or the tomato.
