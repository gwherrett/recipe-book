# Salmon Thai Green Curry

A quick green curry built around **pre-cooked salmon**, with zucchini and bell peppers. The sauce is made first and the salmon is folded in at the very end just to warm through, so it stays moist and doesn't fall apart.

**Yield:** 2–3 servings
**Time:** about 20 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 400–500g cooked salmon, broken into large chunks
- 2–3 tbsp Thai green curry paste (homemade — see [Green Curry Paste (Thai)](green-curry-paste-thai.md))
- 1 can (400ml) coconut milk, not shaken
- 125–250ml chicken or fish stock (or water) — less for a thicker sauce
- 1–2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp palm sugar (or brown sugar)
- 2–3 kaffir lime leaves, torn (optional)
- 1 zucchini, halved lengthwise and cut into thick half-moons
- 1–2 bell peppers, cut into strips
- Juice of ½ lime
- Handful of Thai basil (or regular basil or cilantro)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- Steamed jasmine rice, to serve

---

## Method

1. **Bloom the paste:** Scoop the thick cream from the top of the coconut milk into a pot with the oil. Fry over medium heat for 2–3 minutes until the oil starts to separate. Add the curry paste and fry for 1–2 minutes until fragrant.

2. **Build the sauce:** Add the rest of the coconut milk, the stock, lime leaves, and sugar. Simmer gently for 5 minutes.

3. **Peppers:** Add the bell peppers and simmer for 2 minutes.

4. **Zucchini:** Add the zucchini and simmer for 3–4 minutes until just tender — don't let it go soft.

5. **Season:** Stir in the fish sauce and lime juice. Taste and adjust: more fish sauce for salt, sugar for sweetness, lime for sourness. Do this before adding the salmon so it doesn't need stirring afterward.

6. **Warm the salmon:** Reduce heat to low and nestle the salmon chunks into the sauce. Spoon sauce over and leave for 2–3 minutes just to heat through. Don't stir vigorously or let it boil.

7. **Finish:** Take off the heat, stir in the basil, and serve over jasmine rice.

---

## Notes

- **Heat:** Homemade green paste is often hotter than store-bought. Start with 2 tbsp, taste the sauce, and add more if needed.
- **Zucchini** isn't traditional in Thailand — it's a common stand-in for Thai eggplant. Cut it thick so it holds its shape.
- **Seasoned salmon:** If the salmon is smoked or heavily seasoned, ease back on the fish sauce.
