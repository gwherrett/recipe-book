# Black Bean Salad (Mango, Corn, Chili & Lime)

**Source:** [Kalefornia Kravings](https://kaleforniakravings.com/black-bean-mango-avocado-salad/)

**Prep Time:** 15 minutes
**Total Time:** 15 minutes
**Yield:** 6 servings

A zesty salad loaded with black beans, fresh mango, lots of veggies and creamy avocado, all tossed in lime juice with a touch of spice. Refreshing and perfect for summer potlucks.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 (15 oz.) can of black beans, drained & rinsed
- 2 small mangos, diced*
- 2 avocados, diced
- 1 cup corn kernels (about 2 large ears of corn)
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- ½ of a red onion, chopped
- ¼ (heaping) cup chopped cilantro
- 3–4 limes, juiced (or enough to coat the salad)
- ½ tsp chili powder
- Salt, to taste
- Cracked black pepper, to taste

---

## Instructions

1. **Toss:** Add the black beans, mango, corn, bell pepper, red onion and cilantro to a large bowl. Squeeze the limes over the top and add the chili powder. Season with salt and black pepper to taste then toss the salad until it's fully coated. (I'm very generous with the salt.) Taste again and adjust the seasonings and lime juice, if needed.

2. **Chill:** Cover the salad and let it chill in the refrigerator for 30 minutes – 1 hour.

3. **Serve:** Top each individual bowl with avocado right before serving (or mix it into the entire salad if you're not planning to save leftovers). Serve with extra lime wedges on the side, if desired.
